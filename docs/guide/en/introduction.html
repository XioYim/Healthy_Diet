What Healthy Diet changes

Eating is more than filling a hunger bar: the mod adds configurable nutrition, repeat-eating restrictions and optional temperature-aware shelf life. Server owners define the rules; players can inspect the outcome in game.

01Three connected systems

Nutrition groups and stagesFood can supply several administrator-defined groups. Keeping groups within configured ranges can activate stages, attribute modifiers and potion bonuses.
Fatigue and aversionRepeated eating may trigger a temporary cooldown; lifetime consumption can permanently reduce the benefits of a specific food. Both are configured per food.
Shelf life and temperatureConfigured foods track remaining time. Environment, seasons, refrigerators and dimension multipliers affect spoilage; each item defines its expiration result.

The mod also provides a nutrition panel, item tooltips, quality and rarity, gradients, TitlePro and a local food query viewer.
